# Design intent

A readable baseball front office: light paper, white surfaces, navy navigation, field green and restrained red. Condensed Windows-native Bahnschrift/Arial Narrow headlines create a sports editorial voice; Segoe UI body text and tabular numbers keep evidence legible. No external font or script dependency.

The horizontal department navigation separates club direction, roster construction, scouting, acquisitions, contracts, development and decisions. Lists are compact and paginated. Rotation grades and a defensive diamond explain roster shape visually.

Player reports lead with a strong verdict, a smaller grade, a fuller assessment and the recommended role. A direct organizational comparison explains alternatives and costs. Current ability, future upside and club fit are separate reading sections. Deeper stats, contracts, methods and history use tabs and expandable evidence. Current skill comparisons put inferred positional regulars before the broader MLB cohort; tied ranks and sample sizes remain visible.

Intended position is a GM choice; the department's best fit is an inspectable preference. Standing locks remain binding. Missing evidence receives an explanation instead of a fabricated outcome. The 1–10 grade is a preference score, not predicted wins.

Keyboard controls, visible focus, labeled inputs, semantic dialogs/tables, text alongside color and responsive grids support desktop and narrow screens. The game date and snapshot context stay visible. The companion never advances or edits OOTP.
